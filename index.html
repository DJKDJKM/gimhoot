How to write the lines
Multiple choice — the question, then the correct answer, then the wrong ones:
What is 7 × 8? | 56 | 54 | 63 | 49
More than one right answer — mark each correct one with *:
Which are prime? | *7 | *11 | 9 | 15
Typed answers — use > and list what you will accept:
Capital of Japan? > Tokyo
An explanation — add // at the end of any line:
What is the Sun? | A star | A planet // An ordinary star, very close to us.
